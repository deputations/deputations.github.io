Methodology

Versioned, public, and revised with a changelog. DeFeX is meant to be auditable.

The formula

DeFeX = clamp(0, 100,  B − Σ Pᵢ + E)

B — base score from Deputation_Allowed: Yes 88 · Allow for some 70 · Allowed with conditions 58 · No 18.

Pᵢ — friction penalties: Vacancy-based −18 · Senior-only −15 · FCFS −8. “Highly beneficial to parent” is not a penalty — it's the standard statutory phrasing. We show it as a condition instead.

E — evidence bonus: +8 with an official OM, +2 with consistent community reports (capped at +8).

Missing data does not reduce DeFeX. It reduces confidence.

Confidence, not just score

Every DeFeX score ships with a confidence band. Confidence rises with: number of reports, presence of an official OM, recency, internal consistency, and how many stages (forwarding, NOC, vigilance, relieving) are covered.

  • High ≥ 0.70 — multiple reports + OM
  • Medium ≥ 0.45 — OM or several reports
  • Low ≥ 0.20 — one report, no OM
  • Insufficient — organisation appears as Unrated

Bands

  • 80–100Deputation-friendly
  • 65–79Generally supportive
  • 50–64Mixed
  • 35–49Restrictive
  • 0–34Very restrictive
  • —Unrated (insufficient data)

What we never publish

  • Names of individual officers.
  • Allegations of integrity issues, in any aggregated form.
  • Raw narrative reports — only structured, aggregated signals.

    Disclaimer

    DeFeX is a community- and document-based deputation friendliness index. It is intended to help employees understand patterns, risks and timelines associated with deputation from one government organisation to another. Scores are derived from available reports, official documents where available, and moderated user submissions. DeFeX does not make allegations against any individual officer and should not be treated as an official government rating. Where data is insufficient, organisations are shown as Unrated.
