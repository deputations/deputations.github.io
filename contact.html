Community Guidelines

  • Keep discussions related to deputation, vacancies, service rules and policy.
  • Avoid spam, promotions and unrelated forwards.
  • Verify information before sharing — prefer official sources.
  • Be respectful to all members.
  • Personal numbers, application details and DPC matters: keep them in DMs, not the group.
