DEX — DEPUTATION INDEX

DEX is a score that ranks Ministries, Departments and Cadre Controlling Authorities on how deputation-friendly they are as parent organisations. It measures whether they inform employees about deputation opportunities, forward eligible applications, issue NOC/vigilance clearance in time, relieve selected employees without avoidable delay, and treat deputation as legitimate career mobility rather than as a favour.
